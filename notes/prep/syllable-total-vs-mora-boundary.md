The current source base supports the phonological classification `CV = 1`, `CVC/CVV = 2`, and `CVVC = 3`, but it does not directly prove a millisecond threshold for whole syllable templates of those four shapes (Hayes 1995; Kager 2009; Huehnergard 2011; Greenstein 1984; Kosuke Sugai 2017).

This distinction matters. A summed template such as onset plus vowel plus coda can be used as a modeling heuristic, and the resulting totals line up directionally with the expected moraic classes. But the current perceptual evidence is asymmetric. Stop thresholds concern closure duration, and the best moraic threshold concerns vowel-bearing intervals rather than whole-syllable sums (Hankamer, Lahiri, and Koreman 1989; Kosuke Sugai 2017).

The safest conclusion is therefore limited. Summed template durations are compatible with moraic classification, but the present bibliography does not yet provide a direct perceptual threshold in milliseconds for whole-syllable `CVC` or `CVVC` parsing. In particular, it does not yet provide a direct threshold for the perception of a third mora.
