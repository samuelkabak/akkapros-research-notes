### **1. Function Words (Prepositions)**
In Akkadian, prepositions are generally considered "non-metrical" words in poetry that do not carry independent word stress (John Huehnergard 2011). They typically govern the genitive case (Richard Caplice 2002).

*   ***ana***: A primary preposition meaning "**to, for, at, toward**" (John Huehnergard 2011). It can indicate spatial movement, temporal duration, or purpose (Giorgio Buccellati 1996).
*   ***ina***: A preposition meaning "**in, on, among, by, from**" (John Huehnergard 2011). While primarily locative, it acts as an ablative ("from") when motion away from a location is implied (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***eli***: Meaning "**on, upon, over, against**" (John Huehnergard 2011). It is used in comparisons to mean "**more than**" (John Huehnergard 2011). Before pronominal suffixes, the final vowel is phonologically lengthened (Edward L. Greenstein 1984).
*   ***adi***: Functions as a preposition meaning "**until, as far as, up to**" (John Huehnergard 2011). In mathematical or distributive contexts, it indicates frequency, such as *adi šinīšu* ("twice") (Richard Caplice 2002).
*   ***kīma***: A versatile word meaning "**like, as, according to**" (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***ištu***: A preposition meaning "**from, out of, away from**" or temporally "**since, after**" (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***ultu***: A variant of *ištu* ("**from, since**") used predominantly in Babylonian dialects (Richard Caplice 2002).
*   ***aššum***: Meaning "**concerning, because of, on account of**" (John Huehnergard 2011). It is etymologically derived from the compound *ana* + *šumum* ("for the name of") (Giorgio Buccellati 1996).

### **2. Conjunctions**
*   ***u***: The primary coordinator meaning "**and**" (John Huehnergard 2011). It is treated as an independent word that connects nouns or clauses but remains unaccented (Shlomo Izre'el 2004).
*   ***kī***: Meaning "**how, when, if, that, just as**" (John Huehnergard 2011). In the script, it is often written *ki-i* to prevent confusion with the 1cs possessive suffix *-ī* (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***šumma***: The standard conjunction for "**if**" used to introduce conditional clauses (John Huehnergard 2011). When doubled (*šumma ... šumma*), it means "**whether ... or**" (Giorgio Buccellati 1996).
*   ***inūma***: A conjunction meaning "**when, at the time that**" used to introduce temporal subordinate clauses (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***aššum***: When functioning as a subordinating conjunction, it introduces causal clauses ("**because**") (Richard Caplice 2002).

### **3. Particles**
*   ***lū***: A multi-functional particle that can be **affirmative** ("indeed"), **precative** ("may/let"), or a **coordinator** ("or, either... or") (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***lā***: The negative particle used for **subordinate clauses**, prohibitions, and in sentences containing an interrogative word (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***ul***: The standard negative particle used to negate the predicate of **main clauses** (John Huehnergard 2011). It is occasionally written *u-ul* to denote its monosyllabic status (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***mā***: An Assyrian particle used to introduce **direct speech** (Richard Caplice 2002).
*   ***-ma***: An enclitic particle that functions as a **connective** ("and then") or a **focus particle** (Shlomo Izre'el 2004). It causes the preceding vowel to lengthen and characteristically attracts the word stress to the penultimate syllable (John Huehnergard 2011).

### **4. Pronouns**
*   ***anāku***: The independent personal pronoun for "**I**" (1cs nominative) (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***atta***: The independent personal pronoun for "**you**" (2ms nominative) (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***šū***: Meaning "**he**" or "**it (m)**" (3ms nominative) (John Huehnergard 2011). It also functions as a distal/anaphoric demonstrative for "**that**" or "**the aforementioned**" (Richard Caplice 2002).
*   ***šī***: Meaning "**she**" or "**it (f)**" (3fs nominative) (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***kâša***: The independent **dative/accusative** pronoun for "**to you**" or "**you**" (2ms) (Richard Caplice 2002).
*   ***annû***: The near demonstrative meaning "**this**" or "**these**" (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***šuātu***: An anaphoric pronoun for "**that, the same**" used for both masculine and feminine antecedents in the oblique cases (John Huehnergard 2011).
*   ***ša***: The **determinative-relative pronoun** (John Huehnergard 2011). It can function as a determinative ("**the one of; of**") or a relative pronoun ("**who, whom, which**") (John Huehnergard 2011).
